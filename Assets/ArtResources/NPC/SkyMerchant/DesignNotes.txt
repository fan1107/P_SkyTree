Sky Merchant - separated character and breathing prefab

Prefab: Assets/Prefabs/NPC/SkyMerchant.prefab
Controller: Assets/Animations/NPC/SkyMerchant/SkyMerchant.controller
Animation: Assets/Animations/NPC/SkyMerchant/Idle_Breathe.anim
Preview: Verification/SkyMerchantRig/SkyMerchant-Idle.gif

Nine transparent PNG parts in Parts/: Head, Torso, ArmCoin, ArmLedger,
LegLeft, LegRight, Scarf, Satchel, Hat. The coin stays attached to its hand;
the ledger stays attached to the supporting arm. Shoulder, neck and hip
surfaces include overlap. Hat and head are separate connected transforms.

The prefab uses SpriteRenderers and the existing CaptainSprite material,
with a SortingGroup and an Animator. Its only state is Idle_Breathe,
which starts automatically and loops every 3.2 seconds. There are no
animation parameters or transitions. Feet stay planted; the upper body
rises slightly, the torso expands vertically, and the head, arms, scarf
and satchel have small secondary breathing motions. Root motion is off.
The sole origin is near y=0 and the character is about 3.4 Unity units tall.

Original concept: ComfyUI / Krea2, SkyMerchant-Concept01.png, preserved.
Part reconstruction and transparent atlas: built-in image_gen edit tool,
using the original concept as its reference. Prompt: PartsGenerationPrompt.txt.
Atlas extraction and rig assembly: Unity Editor. No background color keying.

Rebuild with Tools > Sky Merchant > Build Breathing Prefab.
Builder: Assets/SkyTree/Editor/SkyMerchantRigBuilder.cs
This recreates only the merchant's derived parts, animation and prefab.

Verified: 9 sprite parts, no missing scripts, one default looping state,
continuous loop endpoints, fixed feet, and real Animator advancement.
Runtime chest rise: 0.018 units; loop endpoint position error: 0.
The visual preview contains 48 frames at 15 fps, one complete 3.2-second cycle.
