Route selection popup implementation

Prefab: Assets/UIPrefabs/Popups/RouteSelectionPopup.prefab
Art: Assets/ArtResources/Atlas/RouteMap/CloudChartLandmarks.png and TreeShipMarker.png
Atlas: Assets/ArtResources/Atlas/RouteMap/RouteMap.spriteatlas
Rebuild: SkyTree/Build Route Selection Popup (Editor menu, edit mode).

The popup is a 352 x 636 panel inside the existing safe-area 400 x 720 UI frame.
It blocks lobby input; Close hides it without leaving the lobby. Every Open resets selection.
Nine point buttons use 44 x 44 hit areas, with separate sharp vector circle artwork.
Map image, divider grid, route arrow, selection ring, labels and ship marker are separate layers.
The current map adds a monochrome sepia merchant airship at point 3 (top right) and a small floating island at point 4 (middle left). The original CloudChart.png is retained. See Verification/RouteMap-Landmarks.txt for image editing prompts.
Internal point IDs are zero-based. Start is 7 (bottom middle). IDs are not displayed.
Horizontal, vertical and diagonal adjacent steps are supported. Straight destinations keep their direct route; nonstraight destinations use two steps via an intermediate waypoint.
Repeated clicks on the same nonstraight destination alternate straight-then-diagonal and diagonal-then-straight. From displayed point 8 to 3: 8->5->3, then 8->6->3, then wrap.
Changing destination or reopening resets to the first option. Selecting the current point clears selection and disables departure.
RoutePlan.Waypoints stores the source, every crossed cell and the destination as zero-based indices. The drawn route follows each segment.
RoutePlan supplies distance * 2 normal waves and one final boss (updated 2026-11-12). Departure is guarded against duplicate taps.

Integration: SkyTreeGame.SelectedRoute starts VoyageBattleController with the exact selected waypoints.
Three enemies spawn per normal wave; all must die before the next wave starts. Two normal waves complete one cell, and a single final boss follows all normal waves. Hull HP carries between encounters.
The intermediate point is saved after its two waves; the destination is saved only after boss victory. Defeat/return preserves the last checkpoint.
SkyTree.World.CurrentPoint is read with a default of 7 and written on confirmed checkpoints/outcomes. The returned map animates the ship along the traveled route and keeps its marker at the saved point. Reopening and the next departure use this point.
WorldLobbyTable now chooses the lobby by saved world/region: World 1 region 3 uses LobbyView_SkyMerchant, region 4 uses LobbyView_LoneSkyTreeIsland, and the other regions use LobbyView. Actual trading features and voyage rewards remain follow-up work.
Destination labels and arrival descriptions are serialized on the popup and can be edited in Inspector.
